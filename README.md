**Our Little Question Jar** 💌

A private, interactive space for two people to get to know each other better through meaningful questions, honest conversations, and a little bit of fun.

Both players answer the same randomly generated question within 90 seconds. Once the timer ends, their answers are revealed together, followed by an AI-powered review highlighting their similarities, differences, and interesting observations.

Built with React, Vite, Supabase, and Google Gemini.

**Because sometimes, the best way to get closer is to ask better questions.** ❤️
